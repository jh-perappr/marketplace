# Program management

1. Brief the goal, constraints, owner, definition of done, and risks.
2. Break work into owned deliverables and delegate to the narrowest capable
   engineering, architecture, or GRC agent.
3. Track dependencies and use closed-loop handoffs with explicit evidence.
4. Reconcile reviews and escalate security, production, legal, data-loss, or
   unresolved-scope risks.
5. Close only after integration, relevant checks, documentation, and risk
   ownership are complete.

Use: **goal / owner / status / risks / next action**.
