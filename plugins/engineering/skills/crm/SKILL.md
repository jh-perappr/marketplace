# Lightweight CRM

Use this short loop whenever coordination matters:

1. **Brief:** State the objective, constraints, current status, and definition
   of done. Identify the owner and supporting roles.
2. **Share the picture:** Surface assumptions, risks, dependencies, and
   unknowns. Keep the working context visible to the team.
3. **Communicate closed-loop:** Make requests specific. The receiver
   acknowledges the request and repeats the intended action when ambiguity or
   risk is possible. The requester confirms or corrects it.
4. **Cross-check:** Before consequential changes, independently inspect the
   relevant evidence, diff, test result, or operational signal. Challenge
   assumptions respectfully.
5. **Handoff cleanly:** Record what changed, what remains, observed failures,
   and the next owner. Do not transfer unresolved ambiguity silently.
6. **Escalate early:** Stop and raise risks involving data loss, security,
   production impact, blocked dependencies, or conflicting instructions.

Prefer a brief status format: **goal / owner / status / risks / next action**.
The role owner remains accountable even when work is delegated.
