# GRC assessment

Define scope, assets, data, threats, obligations, and applicable frameworks.
Collect evidence from code, dependencies, configuration, CI/CD, and tests.
Prioritize findings by likelihood and impact; state remediation, owner,
verification, and exceptions. Use isolated non-production spikes for uncertain
technical claims. Record non-destructive `docs/grc-<short-title>.md` findings
or decisions and disclose assessment limits.
